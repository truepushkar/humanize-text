If you want to make genuine Indian masala chaita, the first step is to collect the ingredients. Prepare 2 cups of full-fat milk, 1 cup of water, 2 teaspoons of loose black tea leaves, 1 inch grated ginger, 2 crushed green cardamom, 1 small cinnamon bar and sugar according to taste. Add the water and spices to the pot, boil, then add the tea leaves and boil for about 2 minutes. Add milk and sugar and cook with mild heat for another 3-4 minutes. Finally sieve it through the teaspoon, pour it into the cup and drink it hot, so you can have a full and aromatic chai.
