In this city, more and more places create roof gardens. To be honest, small initiatives of this kind have a greater positive impact on the population than on the municipal administration's projects. Three years ago, a friend of mine turned his apartment into a gravel roof tomato basil garden, and now the whole building shares the crop. Children also learned through experience where food comes from, and the rooms on the top floor were quite cool in the summer. It costs the residents little and requires little state intervention.
