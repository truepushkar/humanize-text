## Quality axes (5 the user asked to compare)

Scores 1-5, judged on the English final outputs (T1/T3 are the informative pair
since their inputs were extreme AI-ish styles; T2 input was already human).

- Information Completeness: old 3.5 — dropped/garbled details (T3 turned cardamom
  into "tofu", merged teaspoon/tablespoon, added invented sensory_asides;
  T1 kept all four points). new 4.5 — kept all facts (spices, quantities,
  boil times; T1 all four points), only minor drift (basil→garden, friend's
  roof phrasing).
- Language Fluency: old 2.5 — translation-ese artifacts ("rain after east
  bamboo", "mung beans", "the filling of the milk"). new 4 — readable natural
  English throughout, occasional awkward collocation ("concrete roof tomato
  basil garden").
- Style Adaptability: old 2 — flattened everything into the same measured,
  slightly formal register; list-ified the recipe; kept list even though input
  was prose in T3's case (input was prose, old emitted numbered list anyway).
  new 4.5 — conversational for conversational input, recipe stays a flowing
  narrative, keeps casual hedges ("To be honest", "it's worth a try").
- Readability: old 3 — short but choppy, odd word choices force re-reading.
  new 4.5 — direct, varied sentence lengths, clean paragraph flow.
- Creativity & Impact: old 2.5 — one odd metaphor, no voice. new 4 — small
  human touches (dashes, "honestly", rhetorical structure), voice comes
  through, ends naturally instead of a tidy summary line.

Overall: new prompt wins on every axis; detector agreement improved or held on
all three samples (T1 100→42.3→0, T2 68.3→0→0, T3 100→100→56.1 AI%).
